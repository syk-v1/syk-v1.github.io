Do Trump's posts move defence stocks?

An intraday event study of 3,474 Truth Social posts and 209,341 one-minute price bars. Posts about Iran were tagged as escalating or de-escalating, and ITA's 30-minute move against the S&P 500 was compared with about 17,000 normal half-hours. The method was frozen on the 2025 war and tested once on 2026. The result was a clean null (p = 0.93).
